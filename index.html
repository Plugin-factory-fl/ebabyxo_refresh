About Me: E R Y N N  J O I

Erynn Joi is a dancer, creator, and artist with over thirty years immersed in movement, performance, and live entertainment. Trained across multiple disciplines, her career evolved into professional work at the highest levels of music, television, and large scale live productions.

Her credits include appearances connected to Michael Jackson: This Is It, Oprah Winfrey Farewell Show, and the Super Bowl Halftime Show with Beyoncé.

Grammy AwardsMTV Video Music AwardsBillboard Music AwardsVH1 Fashion RocksLatin Billboard Music AwardsNBA All Star GameThe Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon

Throughout her career, Erynn has appeared in live performances, music videos, and productions alongside artists including Beyoncé, Britney Spears, Jennifer Lopez, Ne Yo, Ciara, Don Omar, Aventura, Missy Elliott and more.

Dance became more than a profession. It became a language and a foundation.

EBABYxo is the evolution of that life in motion. A project rooted in experience and guided by instinct, reflecting a woman who has lived inside the work and now creates entirely on her own terms.

Music 🎸

As EBABYxo, her music is cinematic, emotional, and movement driven. Each release is created independently and designed to be felt rather than categorized. The sound lives in late nights, momentum, confidence, and freedom, serving as a soundtrack for becoming rather than conforming.

Art & Design

Under the name JOIRUSH, Erynn Joi creates bold visual art and one of a kind statement pieces. From oversized pop inspired designs to nostalgic sculptural work, each piece is handmade and rooted in impact, scale, and individuality. Nothing is mass produced. Every piece is intentional.
